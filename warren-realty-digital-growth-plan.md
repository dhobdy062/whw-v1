## Raleigh market implications

Raleigh is attractive, but the marketing cannot rely on generic “hot market” language. Current public indicators show a more selective market:

- Realtor.com reported May 2026 Raleigh active listings of 3,292, median listing price of $465,000, median sold price of $442,500, and median 41 days on market. Active listings were up 11.55% year over year while the median listing price was down 2.72% year over year. [Realtor.com Raleigh market data](https://www.realtor.com/local/market/north-carolina/wake-county/raleigh)
- Redfin reported a May 2026 median sale price of approximately $425,000, down 2.4% year over year, with homes selling in about 34 days and 37.9% of homes experiencing price drops. [Redfin Raleigh market data](https://www.redfin.com/city/35711/NC/Raleigh/housing-market)
- The $750K+ segment is materially different from the citywide median. In the February 2026 North Carolina REALTORS® price-band report, the $750K–$874,999 band had 5.0 months of inventory, $875K–$999,999 had 5.7 months, $1M–$1.99M had 6.1 months, and $2M+ had 11.7 months. [NC REALTORS® February 2026 report](https://www.ncrealtors.org/wp-content/uploads/February-2026-Housing-Report.pdf)
- NAR's 2026 Raleigh outlook highlighted strong income growth, 1.3% job growth, net domestic migration, and nearly 27,000 additional households qualifying for a median-priced home if mortgage rates ease to 6%. [NAR 2026 Housing Hot Spots report](https://cms.nar.realtor/sites/default/files/2025-12/2026-housing-hot-spots-the-markets-poised-for-new-buyer-opportunities-report-12-09-2025.pdf)

Priority geographic content should include Raleigh neighborhoods and adjacent Triangle markets where $750K+ inventory is credible, such as Five Points, Hayes Barton, North Hills, Inside-the-Beltline, West Raleigh, 27607/27608/27614, Cary, Wake Forest, Apex, and selected Durham/Chapel Hill opportunities if he serves them.

## Phase 1: website-first launch

### Phase 1 objective

Launch a polished, credible website in 30–45 days that converts high-intent visitors into consultations, valuation requests, and curated search requests.

### Recommended website structure

- Home: luxury positioning, Raleigh/Triangle service area, proof, featured work, and primary CTA.
- About: agent story, market expertise, service philosophy, brokerage disclosure, and credentials.
- Buy: buyer process, relocation help, neighborhood expertise, and private search CTA.
- Sell: valuation CTA, preparation and marketing process, seller FAQs, and case studies.
- Neighborhoods: 5–8 initial pages with useful local insight, not thin SEO pages.
- Featured properties: only approved listings/content; include clear status and attribution.
- Insights: market updates, neighborhood guides, seller education, and video embeds.
- Contact/consultation: short forms, scheduling, phone/text option, consent language.

### Conversion system

- Primary CTA: “Schedule a Private Raleigh Real Estate Consultation.”
- Seller CTA: “Request a Confidential Home Valuation.”
- Buyer CTA: “Get a Curated $750K+ Raleigh Home Search.”
- Lead form fields: name, email, phone, buyer/seller, target area, price range, timing, consent.
- Automated response: immediate confirmation, booking link, and a human follow-up task.
- Analytics: GA4 or equivalent, Search Console, form events, booking events, call clicks, and UTM tracking.

### Phase 1 deliverables

- Brand direction and lightweight visual system.
- Responsive website design/build.
- Copywriting and on-page SEO foundation.
- Domain, hosting, SSL, email, forms, scheduling, and CRM handoff.
- One lead magnet, recommended: “Raleigh Luxury Buyer/Seller Brief: What $750K+ Buys in 2026.”
- Basic market/area content and 5–8 neighborhood pages.
- Analytics and conversion testing.
- BIC/compliance review checklist and launch QA.

The homepage should feature verified proof early: “Raleigh native,” review count, years in business, selected sales/awards, and a small set of high-value case studies. Do not lead with an unsupported “luxury specialist” claim until the brokerage confirms the designation and preferred wording.

## Social content engine after the website

### Content pillars

1. Raleigh luxury market intelligence: price bands, days on market, price reductions, negotiation patterns.
2. Property storytelling: architecture, design, floor plans, renovations, outdoor living, and “who this home fits.”
3. Neighborhood lifestyle: restaurants, greenways, schools/commute context where appropriate, culture, and local businesses.
4. Buyer/seller education: inspections, appraisal, preparation, offer strategy, relocation, and common mistakes.
5. Agent trust: behind-the-scenes, client experience, vendor network, community involvement, and personal point of view.

### One-to-many production model

Capture one 60–90 second vertical video or one market brief each week, then repurpose it into:

- Instagram Reel, carousel, Story sequence, and caption.
- TikTok/Reel with a stronger hook and direct response CTA.
- Pinterest Idea Pin or standard Pin linking to a website article, guide, or listing page.
- Website insight/article or email snippet.

### Recommended starting cadence

- Instagram: 3 feed posts/Reels per week, Stories on 4–6 days.
- TikTok: 3 short videos per week.
- Pinterest: 5–10 Pins per week using evergreen neighborhood, design, and buyer/seller content.
- Monthly: one downloadable guide or market briefing.

Automation should handle drafting, resizing, caption variants, scheduling, UTM creation, and reporting. The agent or approved operator should retain final approval for claims, listings, prices, client stories, and any content that could create compliance risk. TikTok publishing may require a Business account and platform-approved workflow; design the system so a human approval step remains available.

### Warren-specific production advantage

The initial content system should minimize on-camera friction. Record a controlled source session of Warren for approved voice, appearance, and delivery references; then use that source material for scripts, B-roll narration, virtual-tour intros, market explainers, and alternate short-form edits. A human approval step is required for any synthetic or AI-assisted representation, and the system should never imply Warren personally filmed or visited a property when he did not.

## KPIs for the first 90 days

- Website: sessions by source, conversion rate, consultation bookings, valuation requests, and search requests.
- Social: qualified profile visits, saves, shares, direct messages, link clicks, and email capture—not follower count alone.
- Sales: speed to lead, conversations started, appointments held, active opportunities, and listing/buyer agreements attributable to the system.
- Efficiency: content pieces produced per filming hour and percentage of content reused across all three platforms.

## Important compliance note

North Carolina Real Estate Commission guidance says advertising brokerage services or listings requires Broker-in-Charge consent, the affiliated firm name must appear in the actual advertising, and property promotion requires written owner permission. The website and content workflow should include those checks before anything goes live. [NCREC advertising guidance](https://bulletins.ncrec.gov/tech-corner-2/)
